Bridal Editorial — Shital Gurav Makeup Studio

01 — The Studio

Beauty, Refined to You

Makeup, to us, is not a routine. It is a quiet, considered art — composed around one woman, on one of the most remembered days of her life.

At Shital Gurav Makeup Studio, every look begins with a conversation — about you. Your features, your outfit, the light of your celebration and the personality you want to carry through it. We enhance what is naturally yours, so you look like yourself — luminous, poised and unmistakably you.

From the first consultation to the final touch-up, the experience is unhurried and precise. Nothing is generic. Nothing is rushed. Every detail is considered — because elegance lives in the details.
