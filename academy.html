The Tools of the Craft

Education, Seriously

Not Only a Studio — A School of Craft

The academy exists for one purpose: to train serious artists. Structured modules, guided practice and honest feedback — the same discipline that shapes every look created in the studio.

Whether you are beginning from zero or refining professional skills, training is practical from the very first week. You learn on real faces, with real products, at a working studio.

Structured

A sequenced curriculum, from foundation to fluency.

Practical

Demonstration, guided practice and one-to-one feedback.

Personal

Small batches, so every artist is actually seen.
