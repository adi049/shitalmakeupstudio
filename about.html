Bridal Editorial — The Studio

Our Story

Born From a Belief

Every bride deserves to look like the most beautiful version of herself — not like someone else.

Shital Gurav Makeup Studio was founded on a simple conviction: that makeup, at its best, is a deeply personal service. It begins with listening — to who you are, what you carry, and how you wish to feel on your day.

Today the studio unites two callings under one roof. A luxury makeup studio, where brides and families are cared for with quiet precision. And a professional academy, where the next generation of artists learns the discipline, technique and artistry the craft demands.

Personalised

Designed around you — never applied to you.

Precise

Detail before drama, always.

Timeless

Elegant beyond trends and seasons.

Makeup should never mask. It should reveal — the confidence, the calm, the person beneath the celebration.
Shital Gurav — Founder

Founder — Shital Gurav

The Founder

Shital Gurav

Founder · Lead Artist · Educator

Shital Gurav is the founder and lead artist behind the studio. Her approach is quiet, precise and personal — she believes the best makeup is the kind no one can point to, where a bride simply looks luminous, rested and entirely herself.

Alongside her bridal work, she leads the academy’s training — mentoring students through the same discipline of skin, technique and detail that shapes every look created in the studio.

A finished face is easy. A face that still looks like you — that is the craft.
